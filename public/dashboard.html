BTC Levels

BTCUSDT with volume profile, POC, value area (VAH / VAL), key daily-weekly-monthly levels, period zones and session profiles.

BTCUSDT · Institutional Overview BINANCE
◆

Point of Control

The price level with the highest traded volume in the visible range. Institutional traders watch POC as the "fair value" — price tends to revert to it.

━

Key Levels

Previous daily, weekly, and monthly close/high/low prices. These act as support and resistance — untested levels often trigger reactions when revisited.

▬

Period Zones

Shaded ranges showing the previous period's high-to-low range. Price entering a prior zone signals a potential reversal or continuation pattern.

▥

Session VP

Volume profiles segmented by trading session — Asia, London, New York. Reveals where institutional volume clusters within each session window.

~

EMA Ribbon

Exponential Moving Averages (21/50/200). Fast EMA crossovers signal momentum shifts; the 200 EMA defines the macro trend boundary.

V

VWAP

Volume-Weighted Average Price — the session's true fair value. Institutional desks benchmark execution quality against VWAP. Resets daily.

Order Flow

Real-time BTCUSDT perpetual footprint — volume and aggression at every price, open interest underneath, and signals where flow diverges from price.

▦

Footprint Candles

Each candle is split into price rows. Blue = buyers were aggressive, red = sellers; the deeper the shade, the more volume traded there. Every row shows its volume (BTC); outlined rows with bold numbers are the biggest in view. Purple = candle POC, orange dashes = value area. Tick, Cluster and Text change rows, colouring and labels.

Δ

Bar Statistics & CVD

The bottom table shows each candle's Perp vol and Delta (market buys − market sells); deeper shade and bold = unusually large. Toggle CVD to add the running total of delta — rising CVD means buyers control the tape.

⇅

CVD Divergence

Price makes a higher high while CVD makes a lower high (or a lower low vs higher low) — the move isn't backed by aggressive flow. CVD DIV = confirmed (3 candles after the swing). DIV? 5m (dashed) = forming now on that timeframe, before confirmation; tracked on 1m / 5m / 15m / 1H / 4H / 1D and shown for the timeframe you're on and every higher one (1m chart: all six · 5m: 5m→1D · 15m: 15m→1D · 1H: 1H/4H/1D); the strip at the bottom-left shows which have one forming. Toggle with Div ?.

OI

Open Interest Regimes

L+ price↑ OI↑ new longs · S+ price↓ OI↑ new shorts · SC price↑ OI↓ short covering · LQ price↓ OI↓ long liquidation. Flagged when the OI change is unusually large. On the 1m chart OI is judged on 5-minute blocks (Binance publishes OI every 5 minutes), tagged on the block's last candle.

◆

Absorption & Δ Divergence

◆ Outsized delta with almost no price progress — passive orders soaking up the aggressor, often before reversals. ▲▼ A candle closing against its own delta (green on net selling, red on net buying).

≡

Stacked Imbalances

Black ticks mark 300%+ diagonal imbalances. Three or more in a row form a shaded zone that extends right until price closes through it — these often act as support or resistance on the retest.

BTC Risk

The IVth Legion proprietary BTC risk model — where are we in the cycle?

🔥 BTC Euphoria — Historical Risk Levels Risk —

Formula (IVth Legion calibrated Cowen metric, BTC-locked):
d = ln(P) − SMA250(ln P) → stretch above the long-term trend · raw = sign(d)·|d|1.0·ageDays0.5 → diminishing-returns weighting · risk = (raw − min) / (max − min) → normalised 0–1 over all history. Colour = risk band (blue = accumulate / undervalued, red = euphoria / distribute). Data: daily BTC close since 2011 (blockchain.info). This reconstruction reads ~0.05 below Into The Cryptoverse by design.
