What the Legion Runs On

Not repackaged TradingView scripts. These are proprietary models built from years of live trading — and the person who built them trades with them every single day.

0-to-1 Cycle Risk Score

A single number that tells you where BTC sits in the cycle — accumulation, mid-run, or euphoria. Built on on-chain data and historical fractals from 2018 and 2022. When this reads 0.85+, we scale out. When it's under 0.3, we size up. No guesswork.

Confluence-Only Entries

We don't trade one indicator. We stack 4+ signals — risk score, momentum, key support/resistance bands, and on-chain flow — and only enter when they agree. Most setups get rejected. That's the point.

Every Market, One Thesis

BTC to uranium to S&P to oil. Same macro framework applied everywhere. While crypto-only groups miss the equity rotation and stock groups miss the halving cycle, we trade the full picture.

Every Telegram post comes with an annotated chart. No "BTC looks bullish" one-liners. You see the exact levels, the exact confluences, and the reasoning — so you learn the framework, not just follow calls.
